# Case-008 Courts — Frozen Baseline Closeout

Date: 2026-09-28 (Asia/Tokyo)

## 1. Executive summary

**Case-008 research chain COMPLETE for the frozen baseline phase.** The complete sequence—source survey, selection-protocol freeze, row selection, Ground Truth freeze, and first frozen benchmark—was audited from committed artifacts and found chronologically and methodologically intact.

The source finding is decisive and distinct from the benchmark result: the locked 127-page Courts PDF contains multiple document grammars, so one file cannot safely supply one global grammar, context mechanism, or unit. Freezing a standard-ledger-only universe before candidate inspection made selection and Ground Truth provenance safe. The later benchmark did not demonstrate that staffing, policy-framework, or blank-separator sections caused a target-page failure. Instead, the observed benchmark failures are engine-specific: candidate-resolution ambiguity for pdf.js/PyMuPDF and table-grid association failure for Docling.

## 6. Selection methodology recap

The frozen unit was one physical row in the standard-ledger body. The universe was `pdfPageIndex` 6–110 under organization `010 裁判所`, identified jointly by range, title, repeated standard header, hierarchy, and section-level 千円 declaration.

Cover/TOC, summary, blank separators, staffing, policy-framework sections, and nested local calculation boxes were excluded by grammar—not merely page number. C1 required row-local request number plus expense code; C2 a visually multi-line label; C3 a three-value triple on that same physical row; C4 section-aware identity, context, unit, and provenance. Context could not cross a grammar boundary. Tie-break was earliest `pdfPageIndex`, then topmost fully eligible row; ambiguous or inherited amount association failed.

The frozen GT is standard-ledger provenance, not staffing, policy-framework, or file-global provenance.

## 9. pdf.js analysis

pdf.js produced 40 target-page lines, including the standard-ledger context, wrapped expense label, triple, page, and `千円`. Its normalized output formed the target expense candidate and passed every check except exact item-name resolution. The correct `010 最高裁判所` exists among four item-code-shaped candidates, but the ground-truth-blind normalizer did not promote one candidate to a single `result.itemName`.

Earliest observed failure: **candidate resolution / normalization**, not signal extraction, page identification, expense-row association, or evaluation.

## 10. PyMuPDF analysis

PyMuPDF also produced 40 native target-page lines and successfully reconstructed the wrapped expense row, triple, page, unit, and relationships. Its engine-native spacing differs from pdf.js, but the normalized result and check matrix have the same substantive outcome: the correct item is retained among candidates while `result.itemName` remains null rather than guessed.

Earliest observed failure: **candidate resolution / normalization**, with the same evidence-bounded limitation as pdf.js.

## 11. Docling analysis

Docling identified target page 6, recovered `千円`, and emitted one table with 53 cells and no standalone texts. Its normal pipeline logged a 7×26 grid and reported two of 314 PDF cells dropped. The cells split and merge identifiers, labels, and numeric material across the target region; normalization consequently formed two malformed item candidates and zero expense candidates. It did not associate the target triple with the target request/expense row.

Earliest observed failure: **document/layout understanding (table-grid reconstruction)**. The normalizer faithfully retained null target fields given its malformed upstream structure; it was not changed or used to repair it.

## 13. Multi-grammar causal analysis

The source-level multi-grammar finding is confirmed. The frozen target is specifically in the standard-ledger section; staffing and policy-framework grammars remain in the same file but outside the selection universe.

No raw or normalized benchmark artifact shows staffing/policy material mistaken for the target, nor shows those remote sections causing the Docling grid behavior. **Multi-grammar structure is a source-level fact, but no causal interference with this target benchmark result was demonstrated.** This is not a claim that embedded grammars are unimportant: they were load-bearing for safe universe selection, section-aware unit/context resolution, and provenance.

## 14. Source axis versus benchmark axis

1. **Source/document-understanding axis:** section identity, local units, and context cannot be collapsed to file-global values in this combined PDF.
2. **Selection-methodology axis:** a standard-ledger-only, grammar-aware universe frozen before inspection safely yielded one physical row and provenance-preserving Ground Truth.
3. **Benchmark axis:** observed target failures arise in generic item-candidate resolution (pdf.js/PyMuPDF) and Docling table association—not in demonstrated cross-grammar interference.

## 15. Reproducibility

The evaluation JSON and Markdown differed only in generated/evaluated timestamps; score matrix, counts, and substantive observations reproduced. This is semantic/content reproducibility, not a claim that timestamped files are byte-identical.

## 19. Confirmed findings

- One locked 127-page Courts PDF contains multiple distinct grammars.
- Blank pages are separators; units are section/local, not file-global.
- Standard-ledger-only selection and same-section context resolution support safe physical-row selection and direct visual GT provenance.
- The first frozen scores are 10/11, 10/11, and 3/11; the first two engines retain the correct item candidate, while Docling lacks a usable expense candidate.
- Reproducibility is confirmed at raw/normalized semantic content level.

## 20. Refined or unsupported expectations

- The census header observation is **refined**, not corrected: the apparent exception is explained by section-specific headers and separator pages, not missing ledger headers.
- A file-global unit/context model is unsupported by source evidence.
- The expectation that embedded non-target grammars directly explain the frozen target benchmark failure is **not supported by demonstrated evidence**. It remains an untested possibility for other targets, not a falsified general proposition.
