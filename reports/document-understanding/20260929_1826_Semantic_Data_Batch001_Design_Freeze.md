# Semantic-data Batch-001 Design Freeze

Date: 2026-09-29 (Asia/Tokyo)

## 1. Decision

Batch-001 is approved as a **request-side canonicalization operational pilot with guardrails**. This task freezes its content-blind sampling procedure and authority-isolated orchestration; it does not select or process an authority.

## 2. Evidence and non-actions

The freeze used committed pilot, synthesis, reframe, canonical-protocol, preflight-template, population-freeze, registry/lock-schema, and state evidence only. No PDF/source acquisition, authority content inspection, sampling execution, GT, benchmark, OCR, CSV, MOF work, or production change occurred.

## 3. Alternatives and frozen choices

| Decision | Alternatives considered | Frozen choice | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Batch size | 5, 6, 8 | 6 | Three two-authority waves balance first-batch reviewability and defect discovery without a population claim. |
| Sampling timing | procedure + names now; procedure now/names later | Option 1 | Keeps the rule independent from observing the selected list. |
| Replacement | primary + reserves; no replacement | no replacement | Prevents success-shopping and preserves terminal evidence. |
| Parallel model | one shared branch; parent/child worktrees; isolated authority branches | isolated branches/worktrees from one frozen base | Avoids shared-state conflicts and preserves authority chronology. |

## 4. Sampling contract

The population is the committed 33-authority FY2024 Population Freeze, in its printed MOF-table order. Cases 001–010 are excluded solely for prior exposure. After duplicate and required-metadata handling, the later manifest freezes the first six eligible entries in that order. The JSON procedure is explicit and remains unexecuted (`samplingExecuted: false`). No source or document property is used for stratification.

## 5. Orchestration contract

At most two authorities run in parallel; six authorities form three waves. Each authority follows B0–B10 under the frozen canonical semantic extraction stages. Profile freeze precedes candidate work. Each authority owns separate source, preflight, node/edge, financial, candidate, manifest, and closeout artifacts. Raw engine output, CSV, GT, benchmark, and MOF linkage remain outside the canonical request-side freeze.

## 6. Failure isolation

The ten frozen terminal statuses preserve COMPLETE, partial, NULL, structural, representation, source, ambiguity, extraction, and protocol-block outcomes. Local terminal conditions do not remove an authority. Shared contradictions, unrepresentable structure, provenance/lock problems, leakage, or required shared implementation changes pause the batch and require a separately frozen amendment; no silent repair or replacement is allowed.

## 7. Boundaries and success

Batch success means deterministic selection, terminal accounting for every primary authority, provenance/stage integrity, preserved failures, and auditability. It does not mean all six succeed, estimate population performance, establish MOF linkage accuracy, or prove all grammar coverage. Future request-side readiness labels are permitted as a design slot but are not assigned in this task.

## 8. Integrity and next task

Only Batch-001 design artifacts and state files are changed. Canonical protocol, reframe, synthesis, Cases 001–010, source lock/registry, scripts, parser, normalizer, evaluator, OCR configuration, and production code remain unchanged.

Recommended next task: **Batch-001 Authority Selection Manifest Freeze**. Mechanically execute the frozen procedure, freeze the six primary authority names/IDs and provenance trace, then stop before source acquisition.
