## 7. Selection methodology audit

The frozen Protocol limited selection to organization `010 厚生労働本省`, main standard-ledger grammar, `pdfPageIndex 20–1226`. It excluded summaries, aggregates, non-main nested grammar, and embedded remarks-side structures at substructure rather than page granularity.

C1–C4 required row-local request number plus expense code, a visible multi-line label, a same-row three-value main-ledger triple, and source-safe context/unit evidence. Item context was same-page only; cross-page lookback was disallowed. The ledger unit was section-scoped `千円`; embedded local units could not be inherited. Tie-break was earliest page then topmost qualifying physical row, with a stop at the first full pass and a permitted NULL outcome. The Row Selection record shows these rules were not amended during application.

## 11. Per-engine interpretation

**pdf.js and PyMuPDF.** Both reached page 20, recovered the selected label, same-row triple, unsigned delta, `千円`, and both row-relationship checks. The correct `001 厚生労働本省共通費` is present among four item candidates. The generic normalizer leaves `itemName` null rather than choose among multiple item-code-shaped candidates. The earliest demonstrated issue is therefore candidate resolution, not native-text acquisition or amount association.

**Docling.** It reached page 20 and recovered `千円` from one 99-cell table with two standalone texts. Its row/item material merged into malformed item candidates and it formed no eligible expense candidate. The resulting null label, triple, and relationship fields are downstream of this table/row association failure. The evidence does not show an absence of target source glyphs or a failure to identify the page.

## 12. Scale verdict

**No scale interference was demonstrated in the frozen baseline benchmark.**

The full 1,723-page source processed successfully; target page 20 reached all engines; no target-page truncation, omission, timeout, or resource failure appears in the raw or normalized evidence; and both flat-text engines recovered the target data. This is bounded to this source, pipeline, frozen target, and baseline run. It does not establish that scale never matters for another page, authority, engine, or resource condition.

## 13. Embedded-substructure verdict

The remarks-side embedded structures are a confirmed source property. Their causal contribution to the target benchmark failure is **not demonstrated**. The target page co-occurs with embedded material and Docling has a local table/row association failure, but no generated artifact isolates a remarks-side cell as the contaminant or establishes it as the cause. The relationship remains unresolved rather than inferred.

## 14. Reproducibility and Docling OCR wording

One identical `npm run docbench -- case-010` rerun followed the first completed baseline. All six raw/normalized engine artifacts were byte-identical. Evaluation JSON/Markdown regenerated timestamp-bearing bytes, while observed raw counts, normalized outputs, check matrix, and scores were unchanged.

No separate OCR experiment or adaptation was introduced. The unchanged Docling engine initialized its pre-existing RapidOCR capability as part of normal pipeline behavior. The evidence does not isolate whether that capability materially contributed on this native-text target.

## 15. Limited cross-case positioning

Case-006 establishes a representation boundary: its flat-text engines faced a no-native-text/vector-outline condition, unlike Case-010. Case-008 establishes a combined multi-grammar source and shares the 10/11 flat-text candidate-resolution pattern, but score equality does not prove identical layout mechanics. Case-009 establishes split-package and context-bearing file identity; its 10/11 flat-text pattern recurs, while its Docling 8/11 outcome retained an expense/triple that Case-010 Docling did not form. Case-010 contributes a large combined native-text ledger and a bounded negative result on scale causality.

This is positioning only, not the Case-006–010 cross-case synthesis or a batch protocol.

## 16. Confirmed findings

- Protocol-before-selection, selection-before-GT, and GT-before-benchmark chronology is confirmed.
- The selected physical main-ledger row and visually derived GT are reproducibly located and arithmetically consistent.
- The existing pipeline processed the full source and reached the frozen target page.

## 17. Refined or unsupported expectations

- The planned scale stress-test axis is refined: large document scale is a confirmed source property, not a demonstrated benchmark failure cause here.
- The embedded remarks-side structure is a confirmed document-understanding concern, not a demonstrated cause of Docling's result.
- A 3/11 Docling score is not evidence of text-layer absence; target-page/table association must be considered separately from representation.
